# Burntime v1.1

Hi everyone,

v1.1 is here with a major rework of the AI players and Steam hardware support.

The AI players now play more closely by the same rules as you.
They trade, manage their inventory, plan attacks and retreat from danger.
They also fight back when enclosed or die trying.

The update also brings Burntime to more platforms:

- Native support for Windows, macOS and Linux
- Improved Steam Deck / Steam Machine support
- Keyboard and gamepad controls

Other changes include:

- Rebalanced dogs and mutants
- Music, dialog and gameplay fixes
- More adjustments to remastered graphics
- Autosaves

Note: balancing changes like the dog respawn often apply only in new games.

See the [full v1.1 changelog](https://github.com/jakobharder/burntime/blob/main/resources/Changelog.md#11-2026-09-04) for all changes.

Burntime is a fan project, and feedback is always welcome.
Let me know what you think about the new AI.
Bugs and suggestions can be posted in the [Steam Discussions](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3269080/discussions/).

Thanks for playing!
