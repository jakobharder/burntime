# Steam Store Page

## Short Description

Burntime is a remaster and expansion of Max Design's 1993 post-apocalyptic strategy RPG.
Survive, trade, build camps and compete for control of five cities.
Play with remastered visuals and modern controls.

## About This Game

Pollution and climate change made food and water scarce.
Travel between ruined settlements, scavenge supplies, buy and sell goods, and recruit people to join you.
Establish camps and compete with rival leaders for control of the five cities.

### Features

- **Original or Extended:** Stay close to the original 1993 game, or play with extended content and mechanics.
- **Remastered Visuals:** Play with upscaled graphics and widescreen support.
- **Classic Presentation:** Switch to the original graphics and choose the DOS or Amiga soundtrack.
- **Modern Controls:** Use mouse, keyboard-only, or gamepad controls.

### About the Development

This remaster was created by a longtime fan with permission from the original developers at Max Design.
Its code was written from scratch while retaining the original game's graphics and sounds.

Bug reports and improvement suggestions are welcome on GitHub.

## AI Generated Content Disclosure

The original graphics were upscaled using a combination of traditional non-AI image scaling and Real-ESRGAN-based tools.
Some were manually repainted afterward.

For selected graphics, including character faces, OpenAI image-generation tools were used to reconstruct higher-resolution detail based on the original low-resolution artwork.
The same tools were also used to extend some backgrounds for widescreen.

I reworked the results by hand to make them fit the original artwork.

Development of this remaster began in 2009; Codex has been used as a coding assistant since version 1.1.
