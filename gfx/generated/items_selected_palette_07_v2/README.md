# Selected gameplay items — material-driven v2

This revision gives every item a distinct palette derived from its construction
materials while remaining inside palette 7 and the original item color set.

- `item_tripwire_alarm.png`: visibly reuses the red/gold battery-cell design.
- `item_water_filter.png`: olive pump body, ochre rag filter, violet hose, tin outlet.
- `item_cooking_pot.png`: uneven pale tin body, pipe handles, seams and rivets.
- `item_medical_pouch.png`: folded ochre rags, stitched red cross, violet tie, tin clasp.
- `item_sewing_kit.png`: warm wooden spool, violet thread, cream rag, steel needle.

The cooking pot and medical pouch are intentionally asymmetrical and handmade,
not factory-produced objects. Larger ImageGen renders are preserved in `source`.
