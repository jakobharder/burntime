# Gameplay-oriented item candidates — restrained palette 7

This batch is designed around potential gameplay additions rather than generic
loot. The sprites are 32×32 RGB images on pure black. Every sprite uses at most
24 colors, and every color occurs both in palette 7 and in the original item
sprites.

## Candidates and possible roles

- `item_fishing_rod.png` — camp food-production tool usable at water locations.
- `item_water_filter.png` — improves water output or purifies collected water.
- `item_medical_pouch.png` — direct healing consumable or doctor-class tool.
- `item_lantern.png` — enables dark-room access or improves scavenging yield.
- `item_sewing_kit.png` — repairs clothing or crafts protective equipment.
- `item_cooking_pot.png` — cooks raw food and improves its food/healing value.

Long objects retain the original set's bottom-left to top-right diagonal. Compact
objects use flat front or top views. The `source` directory contains the larger
ImageGen renders, and `preview.png` shows the final pixel-sized sprites.
