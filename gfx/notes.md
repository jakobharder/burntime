# Graphic Asset Notes

Folders:

- original: files ripped from original Burntime
- upscale: intermediate upscaled graphics
- result: final images

v1 vs v2:
- v1 are from the 2024 upscale, and used in v1 of the game
- v2 are the 2026 newly created versions

## Faces

Faces in `result/faces/` are generated by using the `_geometry_true.png` variants from the upscale folder.
They get mask and overlay applied from `upscale/ui/frame-*`, and then downscaled 50% with nearest point.
Nearest point on purpose, as it brings sharper details than a blurred bilinear downscale.

Note: they are currently missing animations. That will be fun to reanimate them again.

## Items

Items in `result/items-v2_nearest-fixed` are the final ones.
They are based on `items` and then retouched in Affinity (repainting black where it got removed).
