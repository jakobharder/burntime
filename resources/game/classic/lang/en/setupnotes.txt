#GAME RULES
Classic follows DOS rules. Amiga uses the original Amiga balance, including its healing, combat and supply rules.
Extended adds equipment effects, food decay and other survival mechanics.

#COMPUTER OPPONENTS
AI is selected separately from game rules.
DOS and Amiga AI follow the original behavior. Remaster AI adds supply, equipment and camp management. AI can also be disabled.

#CONTROLS AND INTERFACE
- Keyboard and gamepad controls
- Contextual prompts and item tooltips
- In-game manual and autosaves
- Treatment preview at doctors
UI hints can be disabled in Settings.

#GRAPHICS AND MUSIC
Original and remastered graphics use the same game rules.
DOS, Amiga and remastered music can be selected independently in Settings.

#FIXES AND CHANGES
Original bugs are not all preserved. See Patch Notes for individual fixes and gameplay changes.
