#GAMEPLAY
- Follows DOS and Amiga implementations as close as possible
- Bug fixes like hazards relying on PC speed
- Modified combat with fairer retaliation; longer-reach weapons strike first
- Trade offer value: 100% / 90% / 80% (a mix of DOS and Amiga rules)
- Optional early victory after defeating the last rival

#QUALITY OF LIFE
- Automatic weapon and protection selection on pickup and before combat; firearms selected manually
- Automatic trap selection in camps
- Doctor previews healing before payment
- Inventory shows damage and protection
- Item tooltips
- More info on the world map: travel days and camp supplies
- Shortcut to show all entrances
- Keyboard/gamepad controls and autosaves

#EXTENDED: ITEMS
- Clothing: 5-25% damage reduction
- Radio: remote camp hazards, defenders and production
- Metal detector: 1% ammunition discovery chance per travel day
- Bible: no food or water use while stationary; normal consumption on trips
- Skull / bones: carrier ignored by dogs and mutants
- Extra equipment: pistol and paper helmet

#EXTENDED: GAMEPLAY
- Doctor: +1 health/day below 50 health for supplied characters
- Food on the ground: decay over time
- New recruits: supplies based on payment and difficulty, rather than always full
- Random starting loot; fewer supplies on harder settings
- Rotating trader stock; limited rare utility items
- Returning recruits: cities replenished first; slower respawns on harder settings
- Dogs, mutants and traders: possible respawns
