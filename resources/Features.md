# Features

## Game rules

| What changes | Classic | Extended |
|---|---|---|
| Starting supplies and loot | Fixed original inventories and loot; regional starts | Random loot; fewer supplies on harder settings |
| Boss starting XP | 37 | =Classic |
| Boss XP gain | 3 per owned camp | =Classic |
| Hiring | NPC XP can be up to 150% of boss XP | =Classic |
| New recruits | Full food and water | Supplies depend on payment and difficulty |
| Combat | Weapon damage table by tier (every 25 XP) | =Classic; clothing reduces damage |
| Defense | - | Best clothing reduces damage by 5–25%; no XP bonus |
| Two-way radio | Trade item | While carried, reveals hazards and reports personnel, defense and production at non-owned camps |
| Metal detector | Trade item | While carried, has a 1% chance per travel day to uncover ammunition after a journey |
| Bible | Trade item | Its carrier uses no food or water while staying at one location; travel still consumes supplies |
| Skull or bones | Trade item | Dogs and mutants avoid attacking its carrier |
| One snake trap | 4 food points/day | =Classic |
| Doctor: one meat | +36 health; treatment tops out at 95 | =Classic |
| Natural healing | 2 health/day from 70; doctor gives 4/day from 50 | =Classic; a doctor also stabilizes supplied characters below 50 by 1/day |
| Barter: your offer's value on Easy / Normal / Hard | 100% / 90% / 80% | =Classic |
| Trader stock | Frequent, broad original restocking | Randomized stock |
| Gas and radiation | Damage over time; unprotected characters also die at the daily check | =Classic |
| Industrial pump at a source producing 3 water/day | 6 water/day | 8 water/day |
| Extra items | - | Pistol, paper helmet |
| Recruitable NPC respawn | Staggered eight-day location cycle; returns to the original location | Staggered 4 / 8 / 12-day cycle on Easy / Normal / Hard; replenishes cities first |
| Other respawns | - | Traders, mutants and dogs may respawn depending on difficulty; mutants may drop loot |

Experience is capped at 99 and can fall when territory is lost. Bosses, doctors and technicians use half their XP for combat; mercenaries use full XP.

**Damage example:** a mercenary with 50 XP and a knife deals **12, 14, 17 or 20** damage under Classic rules. Defender XP and clothing do not reduce these hits. In Extended, a leather jacket reduces these hits to **10, 11, 14 or 16**; defender XP grants no extra protection.

Only Extended maggot production can use a stationed NPC’s carried knife, axe or pitchfork. All other production tools must be placed in a room.

Camp production stops at six stored items of the selected food type; other food does not count toward that limit. Win by holding every camp next to the five cities.

Recruitable NPC spawning uses location cycles rather than one global countdown. Locations are staggered by their map position, and at most one pending doctor, technician or mercenary returns at a location when its cycle is due. Classic restores that NPC at the original location. Extended instead sends returning NPCs to the least-populated city until every city has 12 neutral recruits, then falls back to the original location. Extended spawning becomes less frequent at higher difficulty.

## Computer opponents

AI selection changes how opponents expand, fight and obtain supplies. Original AI uses shortcuts that human players do not receive.

| What to expect | DOS legacy | Amiga legacy | Modern |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cheating | Does not play | Pretends to play | Plays by the rules, with some barter and resource benefits.
| Strategy | Local wandering and opportunistic conquest | Local expansion tied to your progress | Plans routes, supplies, equipment and attacks |
| Recruitment | Free; ignores XP requirements | Free; checks XP | Pays for recruitment; XP check |
| Group | Boss travels alone | Small party; larger garrisons on harder settings | Builds and equips a fighting group |
| Equipment | | | Buys weapons and protection |
| Camps | | Spawns tools, pumps and hazard protection over time | Trades, constructs and builds reserves |
| Supplies | Free boss food and water | Free arrival food and healing; travelling party uses no water | Collects supplies; trades and pays doctors at player rates |
| Combat off-screen | Boss XP and time passed versus defender XP and weapon; attacking gear is ignored and defenders do not retaliate | Party uses carried weapons and fights with retaliation; grows stronger over time | Immediate retaliation; bosses take damage and disengage before critical health |
| Ammunition off-screen | Ignored | Ignored | Consumed |
| Effect on loose items | Deletes one eligible item per maintenance call on Normal/Hard | On arrival, picks up weapons and discards food/some other loot while preserving the current production tool | Collects useful loot |
| Higher difficulty | More consecutive attack turns before a pause | More expansion, guards and combat exchanges | Larger groups, better-armed guards and earlier recovery planning |

## Intentional changes from the originals

- All rules use 25 XP per combat tier; the Amiga original used 26.
- Doctor treatment never lowers health, and empty payment does nothing. DOS uses a 95-health treatment cap instead of the original 96.
- DOS strategic combat uses 1 damage at exactly zero calculated damage instead of 10.
- Classic pumps use the useful original minimum output; Extended adds a fixed 2/5 water. Industrial takes precedence.
- Face 10 has innate gas immunity in all rules, shown as 100% protection even without equipment.
- Water containers are never consumed as food, and Amiga wineskin refills cannot erase camp production selections.
- Gas and radiation use consistent real-time damage rates instead of the originals' machine-dependent timing.
- With no human players remaining, Amiga AI measures expansion against the strongest opposing AI instead.

## Remaster conveniences

- Widescreen support, corrected aspect ratio and original/remastered graphics toggle.
- Sharp or smooth scaling; DOS or Amiga music and Amiga sound effects.
- Keyboard and gamepad controls with contextual prompts.
- Automatic equipment selection, supply warnings, autosaves and clearer inventory information.

Inventory shows damage ranges for all rules, plus percentage defence in Extended when above zero. Map XP dots use full XP in all rules: 1–4 dots for 0–24, 25–49, 50–74 and 75–99 XP.
