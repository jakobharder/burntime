# Features

## Game rules

| What changes | DOS | Amiga | Extended |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starting supplies and loot | Fixed original inventories and loot | =DOS| Random loot; fewer supplies on harder settings |
| Boss starting XP | 37 | =DOS | =DOS |
| Boss XP gain | 3 per owned camp | Grows with food/water output and employees | =DOS |
| Hiring | NPC XP can be up to 150% of boss XP | NPC XP can be up to boss XP + 4 | =DOS |
| New recruits | Full food and water | =DOS | Supplies depend on payment and difficulty |
| Combat | Weapon damage table by tier (every 25 XP) | =DOS | =DOS; clothing reduces damage |
| Defense | - | - | Best clothing reduces damage by 5–25%; no XP bonus |
| One snake trap | 3 food points/day | 4 food points/day | =Amiga |
| Doctor: one meat | +36 health; treatment tops out at 95 | +27 health; treatment tops out at 95 | =DOS |
| Barter: your offer's value on Easy / Normal / Hard | 100% / 80% / 60% | 100% / 95% / 90% | =Amiga |
| Trader stock | More frequent, broader restocking | Eight-day stock cycle; different assortment | Randomized stock |
| Gas and radiation | Damage over time; unprotected characters also die at the daily check | =DOS | =DOS |
| Industrial pump at a source producing 3 water/day | 5 water/day | 6 water/day | 8 water/day |
| Extra items | - | - | Pistol, paper helmet |
| Respawn | - | - | Mutants respawn and may drop loot; dogs respawn on Easy/Normal |

Experience is capped at 99 and can fall when territory or production is lost. Bosses, doctors and technicians use half their XP for combat; mercenaries use full XP.

**Damage example:** a mercenary with 50 XP and a knife deals **12, 14, 17 or 20** damage under DOS and Amiga rules. Defender XP and clothing do not reduce these hits. In Extended, a leather jacket reduces these hits to **10, 11, 14 or 16**; defender XP grants no extra protection.

Only Extended maggot production can use a stationed NPC’s carried knife, axe or pitchfork. All other production tools must be placed in a room.

Camp production stops at six stored items of the selected food type; other food does not count toward that limit. Traders and recruitable NPCs can respawn. Win by holding every camp next to the five cities.

## Computer opponents

AI selection changes how opponents expand, fight and obtain supplies. Original AI uses shortcuts that human players do not receive.

| What to expect | DOS AI | Amiga AI | Extended AI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategy | Local wandering and opportunistic conquest | Local expansion tied to your progress | Plans routes, supplies, equipment and attacks |
| Recruitment | Free; ignores XP requirements | Free; checks XP | Pays for recruitment |
| Travelling force | Boss travels alone; recruits guard camps | Small party; larger garrisons on harder settings | Builds and equips a fighting group |
| Camp development | Stations a recruit to claim a camp | Spawns tools, pumps and hazard protection for free as time passes | Manages production, construction and reserves |
| Supplies | Free boss food and water | Free arrival food and healing; travelling party uses no water | Collects supplies; trades and pays doctors at player rates |
| Off-screen combat | Boss XP and time passed versus defender XP and weapon; no retaliation | Party fights with retaliation; grows stronger over time | Immediate retaliation; bosses take damage and disengage before critical health |
| Equipment | Original loadouts | Original loadouts | Uses owned weapons; buys useful upgrades. Clothing on Extended rules: up to 5% / 10% / 25% on Easy / Normal / Hard |
| Rifle ammunition off-screen | Not consumed | Not consumed | Consumed |
| Effect on loose items | Deletes one eligible item per maintenance call on Normal/Hard | On arrival, picks up weapons and discards food/some other loot while preserving the current production tool | Collects useful loot |
| Higher difficulty | More consecutive attack turns before a pause | More expansion, guards and combat exchanges | Larger groups, better-armed guards and earlier recovery planning |

## Intentional changes from the originals

- All rules use 25 XP per combat tier; the Amiga original used 26.
- Doctor treatment never lowers health, and empty payment does nothing. DOS uses a 95-health treatment cap instead of the original 96.
- DOS strategic combat uses 1 damage at exactly zero calculated damage instead of 10.
- DOS pumps add at least 1/2 water for hand/industrial pumps; Extended adds a fixed 2/5. Industrial takes precedence.
- Face 10 has innate gas immunity in all rules, shown as 100% protection even without equipment.
- Water containers are never consumed as food, and Amiga wineskin refills cannot erase camp production selections.
- Gas and radiation use consistent real-time damage rates instead of the originals' machine-dependent timing.
- With no human players remaining, Amiga AI measures expansion against the strongest opposing AI instead.

## Remaster conveniences

- Widescreen support, corrected aspect ratio and original/remastered graphics toggle.
- Sharp or smooth scaling; DOS or Amiga music and Amiga sound effects.
- Keyboard and gamepad controls with contextual prompts.
- Automatic equipment selection, supply warnings, autosaves and clearer inventory information.

Inventory shows damage ranges for all rules, plus percentage defence in Extended when above zero. Map XP dots use full XP in all rules: 1–4 dots for 0–24, 25–49, 50–74 and 75–99 XP.
